SHAPES – SECTIONS

U-Channels, I-Beams, and Equal V-Angles engineered with high yield strength for pre-engineered buildings, bridges, and heavy industrial structures.

STRUCTURAL STEEL SHAPES & SECTIONS

U-CHANNELS

Sizes: U80, U100, U120, U140, U150, U160, U180, U200, U250, U300. Standards: JIS G3101 SS400, ASTM A36.

  • High torsional and bending resistance
  • Ideal for beams, floor joists, and equipment bases

I-BEAMS (SECTIONS)

Sizes: I100, I120, I150, I198, I200, I248, I250, I298, I300. Exceptional shear and bending moment capacity.

  • Primary column and beam load-bearing members
  • Seamless welding and bolted connections

V-ANGLES

Sizes: V40x40 to V150x150. Available in black and hot-dip galvanized finishes.

  • Power transmission towers and telecom masts
  • Industrial bracings and mechanical frames
