ABOUT VINA ONE

Over two decades of shaping monumental projects, advancing into one of Southeast Asia's premier high-tech steel manufacturers.

ENTERPRISE OVERVIEW

VINA ONE STEEL MANUFACTURING CORP.

Established in 2002, after years of continuous growth and development, Vina One Steel Manufacturing Corporation stands as one of the fastest and most reliably growing steel manufacturers in Vietnam.

Through substantial investments in modern machinery, automated production lines, and an overseas-trained engineering workforce, Vina One consistently supplies high-quality steel products meeting rigorous global standards.

Vina One manufactures an extensive range of steel products strictly adhering to leading world standards including Japanese JIS, American ASTM, Australian AS/NZS, British BS, and Vietnamese TCVN.

With advanced infrastructure, an innovative workforce, professional customer care, and a robust ERP management system, Vina One is certified by Bureau Veritas Certifications under ISO 9001:2015 and honored in the VNR 500 – Top 500 Largest Enterprises in Vietnam.

HISTORICAL MILESTONES

HISTORY OF ESTABLISHMENT & DEVELOPMENT

A remarkable journey of scaling production, technological innovation, and expanding global market footprint.

2002

The Foundation

Officially founded Vina One Steel Manufacturing Corporation, establishing the initial production facility in Tay Ninh province.

2008 – 2012

Capacity Expansion & Section Steel

Invested in automated U-I-V section steel and C-Z purlin rolling mills, expanding the factory campus to 200,000 m².

2015 – 2018

Global Standards & Export Reach

Achieved ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications; successfully expanded exports into the USA, Australia, Mexico, and ASEAN.

2020 – Present

National Brand & 900,000 Tons/Year Capacity

Awarded Vietnam National Brand for 4 consecutive terms, VNR500 top enterprise, total capacity exceeding 900,000 tons/year.
