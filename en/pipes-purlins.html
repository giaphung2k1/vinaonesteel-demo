PIPES – PURLINS

Vina One Pipes and Purlins are produced on fully automated manufacturing lines, offering smooth surface finish, outstanding structural durability, and corrosion resistance.

ALL STEEL PIPES & PURLINS

ROUND STEEL PIPE

Diameter: Ø21.2mm – Ø219.1mm, Wall Thickness: 1.0mm – 8.0mm.

HOT DIP GALVANIZED PIPE

Heavy zinc coating conforming to ASTM A123 / BS EN ISO 1461 standards.

OVAL STEEL PIPE

Widely applied in architecture, precision engineering, and furniture.

SQUARE STEEL PIPE

Dimensions: 12x12mm – 150x150mm, standard JIS G3466.

RECTANGULAR PIPE

Dimensions: 10x20mm – 100x200mm, optimized for structural frames.

GALVANIZED ANGLE

Equal leg angle steel with anti-corrosion galvanized surface.

C-SHAPED PURLIN

Height C80 – C300 with automated CNC hole punching per blueprint.

Z-SHAPED PURLIN

Lap-joint capability for expansive roofing spans with high load support.
