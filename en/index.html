ABOUT VINA ONE STEEL

Established in 2002, through years of establishment and continuous development, Vina One Steel Manufacturing Corporation is recognized as one of the leading steel manufacturers in Vietnam with high and stable growth rate.

With modern manufacturing facilities spanning over 200,000 m² and total annual capacity exceeding 900,000 tons/year, Vina One delivers a comprehensive ecosystem of steel products: U-I-V Section Steel, Round/Square/Rectangular Steel Pipes, C-Z Purlins, and Galvanized & Pre-Painted Steel Coils certified under international standards (ASTM, JIS, AS/NZS, BS, TCVN).
